Recent matches

The collector checks Steam every minute. Steam saves progress every few minutes during a match and adds Bloodpoints only when it ends, so updates are grouped into whole matches. Length runs from the previous match's end (or from launching the game), so it includes the lobby and queue. Rows with a time range were not timed precisely and may hold more than one match.

Built from single matches caught by the minute-by-minute collector. Kill rate = kills ÷ (4 × matches).
